Account Deletion — Tooth Diary

Last updated: October 28, 2026

You may delete your Tooth Diary account and related data at any time. This page explains how to request account deletion, as required by Google Play.

Delete in the app (recommended)

Open the Tooth Diary app → Profile → Settings → Delete account → confirm.

After you confirm, we will delete your account and associated data from our systems. This action cannot be undone.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email us from the address linked to your account:

support@wnhoo.com

Please include:

  • Subject: Account Deletion Request
  • The email address used for your Tooth Diary account
  • A brief statement that you want your account and data deleted

We will verify your identity and process the request, generally within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Account information (including email and profile)
  • Oral care / brushing records
  • Connected device information
  • Synchronization history

Some limited information may be retained where required by law, for security, fraud prevention, or to resolve disputes.

Contact

Privacy or account questions: support@wnhoo.com

账号删除说明（中文）

您可以随时删除 Tooth Diary 账号及相关数据。

App 内删除（推荐）： 打开 App → 个人中心 / Profile → Settings → Delete account → 确认删除。

邮件申请： 使用账号邮箱发送至 support@wnhoo.com， 主题请写 Account Deletion Request，并注明要删除的账号邮箱。 我们一般会在 30 天内处理。

删除范围通常包括：账号信息、口腔护理/刷牙记录、已绑定设备信息、同步历史。 法律要求或安全合规所需的少量信息可能依法保留。
